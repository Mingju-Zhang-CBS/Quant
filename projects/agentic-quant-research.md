**Question:** Can a research workflow learn from an unsuccessful result?

I built proposal validation, past-only factors, date partitions, frozen selection and sealed-stage recovery. The synthetic development winner fails confirmation; that result stays visible.

## Contribution and skills

Research engineering · temporal validation · lineage. This implementation, examples and reporting were independently written for the public portfolio with AI assistance. Original employer/contest sources are excluded.

## Read the evidence

Six proposal fixtures produce three admitted, one duplicate and two rejected outcomes. The frozen momentum candidate has mean confirmation RankIC -0.0707; the negative result is preserved. This demonstrates a process for evaluating an idea, not market alpha.
