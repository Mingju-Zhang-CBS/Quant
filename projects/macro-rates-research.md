# Macro Rates Research

**Policy calendars → instrument exposure → curve risk → funding and evidence.**

[Repository](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/macro-rates-research) · [Methods](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/macro-rates-research/blob/main/docs/METHODS.md) · [Public curve results](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/macro-rates-research/blob/main/reports/curve/REPORT.md)

The public-data study uses actual Federal Reserve GSW curves to compare a DV01-matched 2s10s model position and a three-leg portfolio neutral to two prior PCA exposures. Both retain losses and residual risk. These are hypothetical zero-coupon risk calculations, not account NAVs or Treasury-futures returns.

An offline policy demonstration shows calendar-day EFFR averaging, effective-date exposure, quote-grid conventions and a paired cash/reserve ledger. A separate licensed historical Fed Funds workflow retains vendor-source disagreement and incomplete unit evidence privately. It is not described as a fully verified public-data trading strategy.

Skills: fixed-income mathematics, policy-path interpretation, PCA risk modeling, numerical verification, funding accounting and source judgment. [Course-to-market connections](../cases/class-to-market.md) explain the learning context without republishing source coursework.
