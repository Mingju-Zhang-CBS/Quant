## Six featured studies

### Investing & allocation

#### 01 · [Systematic Strategy Lab](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/systematic-strategy-lab)

**Does momentum survive implementation?** I compare actual public fund NAVs under fixed information, risk-budget and funding rules. The 2020–2025 momentum comparison is negative, and both block intervals contain zero. The study retains all nine cost/delay stresses and explicit vintage limitations.

*Real public NAVs · investment research · allocation and implementation*  
[Case study](projects/systematic-strategy-lab.md) · [Code](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/systematic-strategy-lab) · [Walkthrough](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/systematic-strategy-lab/blob/main/docs/WALKTHROUGH.md) · [Results](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/systematic-strategy-lab/blob/main/reports/real/REPORT.md)

#### 02 · [Financial Text Validation](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-text-validation)

**Does policy text add information beyond prices?** I compare TF-IDF text, price and fusion models on 128 public FOMC statements and first-published EUR/USD references, separating measurement dates from release availability and freezing confirmation choices. Incremental text value remains unproven.

*Public FOMC/FX data · financial NLP · publication clocks*  
[Case study](projects/financial-text-validation.md) · [Code](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-text-validation) · [Walkthrough](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-text-validation/blob/main/docs/WALKTHROUGH.md) · [Results](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-text-validation/blob/main/reports/demo/REPORT.md)

### Markets & products

#### 03 · [Market Making Lab](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/market-making-lab)

### 07 · [Derivatives Pricing Lab](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/derivatives-pricing-lab)

**How do numerical error and hedge costs interact?** I connect analytical prices and Greeks to Monte Carlo variance reduction and common-path delta hedging. The study separates sampling uncertainty, model assumptions and the cost of more frequent trades.

## Choose a reading route

### Investing and Buyside Allocation

Read [Systematic Strategies](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/systematic-strategy-lab) for asset allocation, risk budgets, opportunity cost and funded implementation, then [Financial Text](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/financial-text-validation) for policy-event evidence. ### Sell-Side Quant and Markets

Read [Market Making](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/market-making-lab), [Defined Outcomes](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/defined-outcome-research) and [Pricing](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/derivatives-pricing-lab) for conditional valuation, funding, hedging and model risk. ## Evidence standard

Financial Text Validation uses real public Board text and archived FX references. Systematic Strategy Lab uses official public fund-NAV facts with modeled allocations. Defined-Outcome Research combines issuer facts with modeled scenarios; four other studies use synthetic inputs or simulated paths. Read the complete report and assumptions before interpreting a metric. A valid factor is not an alpha, a synthetic policy ranking is not real profitability, and a local numerical benchmark is not exchange latency. Each project records its own source/data fingerprints and execution environment.
