# Evidence standards

A study is inspectable when its question, information set, implementation, experiment and limitations can be followed together. A test suite is one part of this chain, not an independent validation of every economic assumption.

## Four kinds of inputs

- **Public real observations:** official Treasury yields, FOMC statements, archived FX references and public fund NAV facts. Each source has its own rights and vintage record.
- **Licensed real inputs:** CRSP and OptionMetrics accessed through institutional WRDS. Source records, per-security/per-contract outputs and restricted derivatives stay private. Code and lawful fixtures can be public without publishing the licensed data.
- **Issuer facts and model scenarios:** published product terms support factual descriptions; modeled option claims do not become observed NAVs or executable quotes.
- **Synthetic and simulated inputs:** numerical grids, generated paths and demand assumptions support correctness/mechanism checks. They do not establish real profitability.

## Clocks and evaluation

Observation, assumed availability, decision, implementation, label end and label availability are distinct. Model selection uses training/development information; outer evaluation has a separate role. Historical evaluation is described as historical, including when source periods have previously been examined. A local protocol is not an externally registered trial.

## Units and financial interpretation

Risk exposures, factor returns, residuals, cross-sectional slopes and capital returns are different quantities. A costed basket diagnostic is not a funded account unless its funding, holdings, corporate actions, missing outcomes and execution have also been modeled. Option index points, dollar multipliers and ETF-per-share values are not interchangeable.
